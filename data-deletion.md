# Nightprint - Data Deletion Request

## Delete Your Account In-App (Recommended)

The easiest and fastest way to delete your account and all associated data:

### Steps:
1. Open the **Nightprint** app on your device
2. Tap **Settings** in the bottom navigation bar
3. Scroll down to the **Data & Privacy** section
4. Tap **Delete Account**
5. Read the warning and tap **Confirm**

### What Gets Deleted:
Your deletion request will immediately and permanently remove:
- ✅ Your account and profile information
- ✅ All sleep tracking sessions and history
- ✅ Journal entries and dream logs
- ✅ Bedtime routines and preferences
- ✅ Room environment data
- ✅ Analytics and usage data
- ✅ Device identifiers and push tokens
- ✅ All personal information

**This action cannot be undone.**

## Privacy & Data Protection

Nightprint is committed to protecting your privacy and respecting your rights:

- **Right to Erasure:** You can request deletion of your data at any time
- **No Data Recovery:** Once deleted, data cannot be recovered
- **Third-Party Services:** We will also remove your data from our service providers (Supabase, RevenueCat, Expo)
- **Subscription:** If you have an active subscription, please cancel it through your app store before deleting your account
